\textbf{Pitfalls and FAIRness.} Table~\ref{tab:scans} summarises the scans (service URLs are given in the references). The important OOPS! P10 finding is the scanner's ontology-wide missing-disjointness heuristic and names no affected pair. P34 and P35 concern reused SOSA, SemTS, QUDT, Schema.org, and PROV terms whose authoritative declarations are covered by HCMO's pinned external-vocabulary contract rather than copied into the merged graph; P04, P08, and P22 likewise concern reused BFO or external terms. P13 marks 32 properties as lacking inverse declarations; candidate inverses are not asserted without definition-level evidence, because doing so would create false entailments merely to satisfy a scanner. AskWol's mixed-strategy warning reflects HCMO's stable core-plus-module namespace architecture; the IRIs are not re-minted to satisfy it. The triage reports are archived with the other evaluation outputs\fnurl{https://github.com/dhuzard/HCMO/tree/main/docs/paper/evaluation}.

\textbf{Logical consistency.} The canonical RDF/XML artifact was rebuilt and checksummed again on 24 August 2026 after a metadata-only DOI correction. HermiT loaded 65 classes and reported zero inconsistent classes. The pre-check found no active \nolinkurl{UNKNOWN:} IRI and no property declared as both object and datatype property.

\textbf{SHACL validation.} The validator supplies the canonical ontology as a separate ontology graph and enables RDFS entailment when validating each example, so that domain, range, and subclass consequences select the same \nolinkurl{sh:targetClass} constraints as explicit types. Three positive examples conform, while two edge-case examples produce their reviewed violations. One negative fixture deliberately omits explicit \nolinkurl{rdf:type}: it conforms when validated without the ontology and fails when ontology-aware inference selects the intended targets. This keeps profile requiredness in SHACL rather than in OWL existential semantics. A separate ISA/STATO evidence shape validates the workflow boundary of Sect.~5; an injected process/data cycle is required to fail. \new{For the round-trip fixture, dedicated shapes and injected probes reject inconsistent housing assignments, replacement animals, observation/housing and group/factor mismatches, and fabricated ISA assignment results.}

\textbf{Competency questions.} Eleven SPARQL queries run over the ontology plus all positive examples, and the validator checks the complete expected answer rows, including bindings, unbound values, and multiplicity. For the representative temporal-housing question of Sect.~3, the query returns the reviewed subject, enclosure, and housing-assignment IRI at the stated reference time, exactly matching its single expected answer. All eleven queries match their complete versioned answer sets. The zero-row dimensions query is intentional: it verifies the absence of missing records rather than being accepted as an unexamined empty result. Five further exact-answer questions run against the isolated round-trip fixture of Sect.~5: nine housing-history rows, eight authoritative subject/group/factor rows, eight per-animal repeated-observation counts, four statistical-result/file-fragment rows including exact values and meaning links, and one Source-to-derived-Sample row.

\textbf{Potential impact.} \new{HCM exports are often weakly linked to their subject, enclosure, environment, device, and protocol} \cite{bains2025,forrest2026,petit2026wellfair}. By making the provenance and experimental context of each measurement explicit, HCMO can support dataset discovery, cross-system comparison, integration, and reuse, provided that datasets are mapped to the ontology and exposed with appropriate identifiers and metadata. In animal research, where data are the durable output of an experiment, preserving their context also has ethical relevance: it can reduce avoidable duplication, enable secondary analyses, and increase the scientific value obtained from each animal, supporting Reduction and Refinement \cite{petit2026wellfair}. \new{For AI-supported analysis, it supplies admissible types and relations that can be enforced through validation, ontology-aware retrieval, and type-constrained query generation, though it does not by itself prevent erroneous inference.}

\textbf{Adoption path.} Development is grounded in a network of preclinical and HCM experts originating in COST Action TEATIME, which provides domain feedback and a route to real datasets for validation and uptake. Because the release is recent, longitudinal user and deployment statistics are not yet available; adoption is therefore presented as a pathway rather than a demonstrated outcome.
