\section*{Acknowledgements}
\textbf{Funding.}
Preliminary mapping and implementation work on HCMO was supported by a COST
mobility grant (EUR~750) awarded to Damien Huzard for the project
\emph{Mapping the Home-Cage Monitoring Ontology to Device Metadata}\new{, with
contributions from Leonardo Restivo}. The subsequent substantial redevelopment
of HCMO was supported by an Exogene grant (EUR~23,000), administered by the
Pôle Universitaire d'Innovation (PUI) of the University of Montpellier and
awarded to Metadatapp to support collaboration between Damien Huzard and
Konstantin Todorov. As part of an Exogene-funded Master's project, Cyril
Gilbert redesigned and implemented the HCMO version presented in this article.

\textbf{Provenance.}
\new{HCMO builds on the community-developed HCM Definition Olog (Sect.~2). From
March 2025, Damien Huzard developed an earlier machine-actionable ontology,
HCM-D, published on BioPortal and no longer maintained, within Metadatapp's
semantic-development work. The COST mobility-grant phase supported preliminary
device-metadata mapping, project scoping, and an early GitHub version in
September 2025. From October 2025 to October 2026, the present author team
comprehensively redesigned and formally evaluated HCMO, producing the resource
reported here.}
