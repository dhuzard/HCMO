## Conclusion

No semantic blocker was found in the implemented choices or deprecations. The
review accepts the current policies for the HCMO 0.3.0 release candidate, with their stated
scope limits. In particular, the evidence supports selective semantic reuse and
tested interoperability; it does not justify a broader claim of formal ISA,
SemTS, or QUDT conformance.

No domain-term axiom was changed during this review. The subsequent 0.3.0
release-candidate promotion changed only ontology-header annotations and
version-bearing documentation; no class or property IRI changed.

## Reviewed decisions

- **ISA/STATO:** stable animal identity, time-bounded housing assignments,
  distinct biological Source and derived Sample, and separation of semantic
  STATO results from Files/file fragments are coherent. The extended ISA
  RO-Crate is the lossless exchange fixture. ISA-JSON/ISA-Tab remain explicit,
  tested controlled-loss projections and do not fabricate animal Samples.
- **SOSA/SemTS:** the normative sensing edition is consistently SOSA 2017 and
  uses `sosa:ObservableProperty`. SemTS 1.2.0 entity IRIs are canonical and
  unversioned; `semts:segmentDimension` is used only for time-series segments.
  The removed dimension and `semts:generated` uses did not match their declared
  semantics.
- **Environment and time:** environmental profiles, target specifications, and
  observations are distinct layers. Housing, operation, and calibration are
  represented through time-bounded records and evidence-generating activities;
  deprecated timeless shortcuts remain available for migration only.
- **QUDT:** the pinned 3.4.0 `QuantityValue`/`numericValue`/`hasUnit` pattern is
  appropriate for dimensions, frequencies, specified values, and observation
  results. HCMO role properties state what each quantity describes. This is
  selective reuse, not full QUDT conformance.
- **Claim strength:** the documentation now consistently separates implemented
  semantic reuse, executable interoperability evidence, and formal profile
  conformance. Formal ISA conformance remains deferred because of the draft
  profile identifier and the isolated upstream RO-Crate 1.1/1.2 profile conflict.
