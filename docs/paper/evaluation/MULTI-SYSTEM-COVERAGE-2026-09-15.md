## Competency questions on instance data

Fourteen cross-system questions (`evaluation/queries/`) run over the union of
the ontology and the four graphs (36,648 triples). Twelve are fully answered,
one has a verified empty answer, and two are partial for a documented reason:

procedure, so the dark-phase and procedure-provenance questions return rows for
the three other systems only.

## Finding fixed during the evaluation

The `hcm:RejectedTimelessStateShape` SPARQL constraint used a `VALUES` clause,
which the SHACL specification forbids in SPARQL-based constraints. pySHACL
returned a validation *failure* for any graph containing the deprecated
booleans, and the gate had been counting that failure as non-conformance. The
constraint was rewritten with an equivalent `FILTER (?property IN (...))`; the
edge-case fixture now fails with 18 genuine violations.

## Lessons for 0.4.0 (from `coverage.md` gaps)

1. No observation subtype for numeric activity, metabolic or operant results
   about a subject or group; generic `sosa:Observation` used (most "partial").
2. `hcm-bio:ExperimentalGroup` cannot carry strain/sex; cage-level systems
   without animal identity need group-level attributes.
3. `hcm:locatedIn` (rack position) and arena zones are timeless.
4. No subject/device identifier property (`dcterms:identifier` reused); no
   cumulative-vs-interval distinction; no trial/session entity in the core.
